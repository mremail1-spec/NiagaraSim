Read this with care

  • A single forecast can be off by about × either way: that is how far the model misses a chain when the chain is left out of its fitting. Comparing spots and kinds is more reliable than the dollar figures.
  • Groceries clear the sales test but keep thin margins (about 1–2% after rent). A pharmacy keeps about 7%.
  • The model knows the businesses on the map, the City's licences and the Region's employment survey, and leaves out those now closed. A competitor that opened recently and is on none of them is not counted.
  • Every scenario matches the chains' published sales per store, Niagara Parks' revenue, hotel occupancy, road traffic counts and Statistics Canada's sales by type of store.
