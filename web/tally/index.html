How to count

  • Stand still and pick a line across the sidewalk or road. Count everyone who crosses it, both directions.
  • People: walkers, people on wheelchairs and scooters. Not cyclists on the road.
  • Cars: cars, vans and pickups in all lanes. At the Falls lot, count cars turning in.
  • Best spread: a weekday and a weekend, around noon and around 6 pm, in summer and again in winter.
